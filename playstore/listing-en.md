## Full description

Explore cities and landmarks across the archipelago in Monopoli Indonesia, a
digital board game combining property strategy with a colorful Indonesian
setting.

Roll the die, move your character, buy properties, collect rent, construct
buildings, and manage your money carefully. Build the strongest property
collection and outlast your opponents to win the match.

Key features:

• A game board inspired by Indonesian cities and landmarks  
• Local two-player mode on one device  
• Online multiplayer for two to four players  
• Properties, utilities, taxes, jail, chance, and community cards  
• Animated character movement with a following camera  
• Sound effects for dice, movement, transactions, cards, and victories  
• Distinctive characters including Komodo, Becak, Garuda, and Kapal  
• Indonesian and English language support

Plan your strategy, grow your property collection, and compete with friends in
an Indonesian-themed digital board game.
